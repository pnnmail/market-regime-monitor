7. HOW TO READ THIS THEORY
--------------------------------------
Normal risk-on:
SPY rising + credit healthy + VIX low + TLT shows no strong outperformance.

Early warning:
TLT starts rising and outperforming IEF/SHY, but SPY and credit are still strong.

MACRO DISLOCATION:
TLT rising + 10Y yield falling + SPY stays strong + VIX low + credit healthy.

MACRO STRESS:
Treasuries strong + equities start weakening + credit deteriorating.

RISK-OFF:
Treasuries strong + equities weak + credit weak + VIX high.

IMPORTANT:
MACRO DISLOCATION does not automatically mean a market decline.
It is a signal that the bond market may be seeing a change in future economic conditions before the equity market.

The main question after a dislocation is detected:
Will credit and VIX confirm this move?

If credit stays strong and VIX low, it may simply be an expectation of lower rates or inflation.

If credit starts falling and VIX rises, the divergence turns into a more serious MACRO STRESS.

If SPY then loses the 50DMA and then the 200DMA, the hypothesis gets confirmation from equities as well.
